If macros are missing, create them in the macro window:

SwiftSetAssist:
  /script SwiftAssist.SetAssist()

SwiftAssistMe:
  /script SwiftAssist.AssistMe()

SwiftAssist (inactive until you SetAssist; SetAssist rewrites this to /assist Name):
  /script SwiftAssist.Inactive(L"assist")

SwiftSmartAssist (inactive until smart assist picks someone):
  /script SwiftAssist.Inactive(L"smart")

After SetAssist, SwiftAssist must look like:
  /assist PlayerName
(not /script SwiftAssist.Assist())
